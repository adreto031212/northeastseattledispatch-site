This weekend, only 1 Line trains stop at U District and Roosevelt

October 17, 2026 · Northeast Seattle Dispatch, issue 1 · All Northeast Seattle news

Shuttle buses replace 2 Line trains between South Bellevue and International District/Chinatown stations today and tomorrow, October 17 and 18, and no 2 Line trains run north of there to Lynnwood. 1 Line trains run every eight minutes; for Bellevue or Redmond, ride to International District/Chinatown and change to the shuttle.
