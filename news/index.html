What changed in Northeast Seattle.

Every story the Northeast Seattle Dispatch has run, newest first. Each links the public record it came from.

November 12, 2026

The council's budget committee starts voting tomorrow, Friday, November 13, at 9:30 AM

More committee votes follow on November 16 and 19, and the final vote is Friday, November 20.

Seattle Forest Week runs through Sunday, and this afternoon it plants at Magnuson Park

Volunteers put in native trees, shrubs and groundcovers from 12:30 to 3:30 PM on the north cross-park trail, just west of the crest of Kite Hill.

James Turrell's Skyspace at the Henry Art Gallery reopens Saturday, November 14

Light Reign is the Henry's permanent installation, and the museum is unveiling it to the public that day.

your block is yours to shovel

SDOT ran its snow-plow practice on November 2 and 3.

November 5, 2026

The budget hearing is today, with remote comment at 1 PM and in-person comment at 5 PM

The Budget Chair's balancing package was scheduled for Wednesday, November 4, at 9:30 AM. Committee votes follow on November 13, 16 and 19, and the final vote is November 20.

plan the Thanksgiving week's trash

In 2024 the city skipped collection on Thanksgiving Day and moved the rest of that week's pickups one day later.

October 29, 2026

Clocks go back one hour at 2 AM Sunday, November 1

Daylight saving time ends: 2 AM becomes 1 AM and that hour repeats.

The second half of 2026 property tax is due Monday, November 2

A mailed check counts if it is postmarked by the due date; online payment carries a service fee.

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3

King County takes ballots three ways: by mail, at a ballot drop box, or in person at a vote center.

The budget chair's balancing package lands Wednesday, November 4, and the public hearing is Thursday, November 5

Remote comment starts at 1 PM and in-person comment at 5 PM in the Council Chambers at City Hall.

Street parking by text is on its way

SDOT started text-to-pay in Pioneer Square and plans it for every paid parking area in the city by early 2027: text PAY to 77447, enter the zone number on the sign, and pay through the link that…

report the dark streetlight

From Sunday the evening walk home starts an hour darker, and a streetlight out on your block shows.

October 22, 2026

Councilmembers bring their budget amendments to the table Tuesday through Thursday, October 27 to 29

Sessions start at 9:30 AM each day.

Leaves get free pickup all November: up to 10 extra bags on each collection day

Seattle Public Utilities takes them with your food and yard waste service, leaves only, no food.

The University Branch library on Roosevelt Way NE now expects to reopen in December, not September

The branch has its seismic retrofit done, plus a new heating and cooling system, an elevator, accessible restrooms, a new service desk and a renovated meeting room.

You can ask for a public EV charger at the curb near your home

City Light and SDOT are taking applications through fall 2027 for up to 200 curbside Level 2 chargers by 2030.

Coming home from the Eastside late? Two SR 520 exits close at night next week

Monday to Thursday, October 26 to 29, 10 PM to 6 AM, the westbound off-ramps to northbound I-5 and to East Roanoke Street close for Montlake work.

clear the drain at the curb

Leaves on the street clog storm drains and pipes, and the water ponds on the block.

SDOT chose a one-mile stretch between NE 74th Street and Matthews Avenue NE; the exact blocks come later.

The council is working through a $9.1 billion budget; the next public hearing is November 5

Councilmembers discuss amendments October 27 to 29 and the chair's balancing package arrives November 4.

Close your block for Halloween, free, if you apply by Tuesday

SDOT's Trick-or-Streets permit closes up to three blocks in a row to cars for an event open to the public, any day October 21 to November 2, until 10 PM; cross streets stay open.
