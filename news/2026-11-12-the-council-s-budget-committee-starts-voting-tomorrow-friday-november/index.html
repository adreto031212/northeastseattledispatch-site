The council's budget committee starts voting tomorrow, Friday, November 13, at 9:30 AM

November 12, 2026 · Northeast Seattle Dispatch, issue 5 · All Northeast Seattle news

More committee votes follow on November 16 and 19, and the final vote is Friday, November 20. Budget Chair Dan Strauss's balancing package, released November 3, puts $352.7 million into housing next year, $5 million above the mayor's proposal, with nearly $7 million for safe routes to school and $7 million for smaller street-safety fixes citywide. The council's page says the schedule is subject to change. Council budget calendar · Budget chair's release
