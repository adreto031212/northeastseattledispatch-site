Questions people ask

Who writes it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publisher. Every item comes from Northeast Seattle's own public record (council agendas, permit notices, school district and transit updates, parks calendars) and links its source. It is researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

How often, and is it really free?

Once a week, Thursday at 6 AM, about five minutes to read. Free, no paywall, unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Why pick a neighborhood?

One section of every issue is written for a specific Northeast Seattle neighborhood. Picking yours means that section is about your streets. You can change it any time.
