Thu Nov 26 – Fri Jan 1 · Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day

Sound Transit holiday schedules

Link and ST Express run Sunday schedules on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day; on Nov 27 Link runs a Saturday schedule and ST Express a weekday one.

Link light rail and ST Express · Regular fares · Organizer's page

Sat Dec 12 · 4:30 to 7:30 PM

Pathway of Lights at Green Lake

Thousands of candles line the path around the lake, with music stages, refreshments and lit hot air balloons if the weather allows.

Green Lake Park loop, paths and docks · Free; bring a non-perishable food donation if you can · Organizer's page

Sat Dec 19 · Kids' dash 9:45 AM; 5K, 10K, 15K at 10 AM

Holiday Fun Run at Magnuson Park

A holiday-themed run on the park's flat paths along the lake.

Warren G. Magnuson Park · $32 5K, $37 10K, $42 15K, $6 kids' dash · Organizer's page

Sat Dec 19 · 5:35 to 5:55 PM

Christmas Ship at Warren G. Magnuson Park

The Argosy Christmas Ship stops offshore for about 20 minutes while its choir sings carols over a speaker for people on the beach. Seattle Parks runs a free bonfire at this stop.

Warren G. Magnuson Park · Free to watch from shore · Organizer's page
