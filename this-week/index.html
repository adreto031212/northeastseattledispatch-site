This week in Northeast Seattle.

The dated things to know between now and next Thursday, from issue 5 of the Northeast Seattle Dispatch. Every item links its public source.

What the city decided and what changed

The council's budget committee starts voting tomorrow, Friday, November 13, at 9:30 AM

More committee votes follow on November 16 and 19, and the final vote is Friday, November 20.

Seattle Forest Week runs through Sunday, and this afternoon it plants at Magnuson Park

Volunteers put in native trees, shrubs and groundcovers from 12:30 to 3:30 PM on the north cross-park trail, just west of the crest of Kite Hill.

James Turrell's Skyspace at the Henry Art Gallery reopens Saturday, November 14

Light Reign is the Henry's permanent installation, and the museum is unveiling it to the public that day.
